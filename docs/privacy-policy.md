This policy explains what information the Puzzaro app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what

data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Summary

- You don't need an account, and we never ask for your email, real name or contacts.
- Your games, statistics and settings are stored on your device.
- To sync your progress and post daily scores, the app signs in with an **anonymous** ID.
- You can delete all of your data, on the device and in the cloud, from **Settings → Clear all data**.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Purpose | Where it's stored | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID (random, created by Firebase Authentication) | Link your cloud save and daily scores to this app installation | Google Firebase | Performance of the service you request |

| Purchase information ("Remove ads" status, an anonymous purchase ID) | Unlock and restore your purchase | RevenueCat, Apple App Store / Google Play | Performance of a contract |
| Settings (theme, sound, haptics, reminder time, game options) | Remember your preferences | Only on your device | Legitimate interest |

- Data on your device stays until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app.
- Cloud data (save and daily scores) stays until you choose **Settings → Clear all data**, which
  deletes your cloud save, the daily scores submitted from your device, and your anonymous account.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to object to or
restrict processing. The quickest way to delete everything is **Settings → Clear all data**. For any
other request, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Because we only know your anonymous ID, we may ask you
to include the leaderboard name you used so we can find your data. You can also complain to your data
protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 [or the age required in your country], and we don't
knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact
us and we will delete it.
