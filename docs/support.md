# Puzzaro Support

Need help with Puzzaro, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** [info@techtable.nl](mailto:info@techtable.nl) — please mention "Puzzaro", your device
and, if it's about a puzzle, the date and game.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone?**
Yes. Every player gets the same Sudoku, Word Guess and Mahjong puzzle each day. A new set starts at
midnight UTC.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to the cloud with an anonymous ID, so it
comes back after a reinstall — no sign-up needed.

**How do I keep my streak?**
Win at least one daily puzzle every day. Your streak and statistics are under the **Stats** tab.

**How do I delete my data?**
Go to **Settings → Clear all data**. This removes everything from your device and the cloud. See
the [privacy policy](privacy-policy) for details.

**The reminder doesn't show up.**
Make sure notifications for Puzzaro are allowed in your phone's settings, and that the daily
reminder is switched on in **Settings**.
